Field guide

  • Move: WASD or touch arrows; hold Shift to sprint. Stamina regenerates when you slow down.
  • Find fuel: hold right-click or touch Flashlight to reveal cans; the battery recharges when off.
  • Fight: aim and hold left-click or touch Fire; press R to reload and collect ammo boxes.
  • Mission: collect 15 cans, load them at the rocket, then press E to launch. Time or rocket health reaching zero means you lose.
  • Interact: press E at cannons to return or at an unlocked ladder to climb. Q uses your ability; Time Stop uses E away from interactable spots.
  • Progress: successful runs earn moon rocks and trophies for the shops. In a boss raid, survive 10 waves and defeat the boss.
